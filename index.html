Frequently Asked Questions

Does this break native two-finger Spaces swipe?

Not at all! macOS native two-finger horizontal swipe left and right for Spaces navigation remains 100% functional and responsive. Magical Touch only handles the missing gestures Apple omitted.

What happens when the 14-day Pro trial ends?

Silent tap-to-click, 2-finger right-click, 3-finger middle click, active boundary tuning, sensitivity sliders, and menu bar controls remain 100% free forever. If you decide not to upgrade, you simply lose the Pro gestures (3-finger drag, CAD orbit, window swipes, and pinch zoom/rotate).

Which Macs and mouse models are supported?

Magical Touch requires macOS 12 Monterey or newer on Apple Silicon Macs (M1, M2, M3, M4 series). It works with Apple Magic Mouse 1, Magic Mouse 2, and the latest USB-C Magic Mouse.

How does tap-to-click prevent accidental clicks?

Magical Touch includes customizable touch sensitivity thresholds and adjustable active surface boundaries. You can tailor the contact area and timing sliders so resting your fingers does not trigger false clicks.

What window management actions can I assign to three-finger swipes?

You can assign 8 actions: Mission Control, App Windows Exposé, Show Desktop, Fill / Maximize, Full Screen, Minimize Window, Snap Left, and Snap Right. Plus, holding Option (⌥), Command (⌘), Control (⌃), or Shift (⇧) triggers 2 additional secondary actions without changing defaults.

How does CAD and 3D Orbit mode work?

In Settings under Three-Finger Drag Mode, switch to 'CAD / 3D Orbit'. Instead of a standard left-drag, three-finger dragging sends a continuous middle-mouse button drag (Button 2) directly to Blender, Maya, Fusion 360, or Unity—allowing single-handed 3D viewport rotation without touching modifier keys.

Does Magical Touch support trackpad pinch to zoom and rotate?

Yes! Pro includes native trackpad-grade pinch-to-zoom and two-finger twisting rotation. It works seamlessly across macOS apps like Safari, Preview, Photos, and Apple Maps.
