Frequently Asked Questions

Does this break native two-finger Spaces swipe?

Not at all! macOS native two-finger horizontal swipe left and right for Spaces navigation remains 100% functional and responsive. Magical Touch only handles the missing gestures Apple omitted.

What happens when the 14-day Pro trial ends?

Silent tap-to-click, 2-finger right-click, and sensitivity tuning remain 100% free forever. If you decide not to purchase Pro, you will simply lose the 3-finger drag and window swipe gestures.

Which Macs and mouse models are supported?

Magical Touch requires macOS 12 Monterey or newer on Apple Silicon Macs (M1, M2, M3, M4 series). It works with Apple Magic Mouse 1, Magic Mouse 2, and the latest USB-C Magic Mouse.

How does tap-to-click prevent accidental clicks?

Magical Touch includes customizable touch sensitivity thresholds and palm-rejection resting zones. You can tailor the contact area so resting your fingers does not trigger false clicks.
